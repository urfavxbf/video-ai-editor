## Visual design DNA

1. **Composition:** solid nearly-black outer canvas; ~986 × 910 px inset with softly rounded corners, a little above canvas center. Use responsive inset proportions rather than blindly rendering text at 1080 × 1920 full-bleed.
2. **Illustration:** eclectic 2D hand-drawn/editorial collage, silhouettes, cutout figures, uneven strokes, flat expressive colors, paper/grain texture. Every few seconds the illustration metaphor changes. Do **not** extract or redistribute the source images for new releases.
3. **Color rhythm:** light ivory/ink opening → dark gray → red/purple urgency → ochre optimism → cobalt/navy work → red warning → yellow/pink breakthrough → charcoal chaos → cool cyan/blue reflection. The repeated change of palette carries the narrative; there is no single fixed background color.
4. **Type hierarchy:** extremely heavy uppercase sans-serif for emotionally important nouns/verbs, usually white or black; supporting clauses much smaller. Occasionally lowercase casual words and blurred entry states. Likely style family: extra-bold grotesk/display sans, **not a verified font identification**.
5. **Kinetic text:** successive word-group replacements, abrupt scale/opacity changes, brief soft-focus/blur-to-sharp reveals, occasional text stretching/cropping and layered text over the subject. These are observations; exact easing curves and keyframe durations are **not reconstructible** from this coarse sampling.
6. **Editing:** section cuts roughly every **1–4 s**, with short rapid punches around 24–29 s and more contemplative 3–4 s passages elsewhere. Most attention comes from text re-composition **inside** a shot, not merely external transitions.
7. **Narrative visuals:** each metaphor matches an idea: pot/flowers for outgrowing, desk for late nights, weight/feet for pressure, cramped house for old life, mirror for standards, brain for exhaustion, heart for doubt, crowded birds for unfamiliar rooms.
