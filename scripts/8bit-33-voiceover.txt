Fade in reveals text. Fade out makes it disappear. Slide left moves the headline across. Slide right reverses it. Slide up rises; slide down drops. Zoom in gets closer, zoom out pulls away. Bounce springs. Rotate turns; spin goes wild. Flip X mirrors across, and flip Y turns upside down. Shake adds impact, wobble sways, pulse beats, and blink flashes. Typewriter draws letters individually. Pop up enters fast. Elastic stretches, then settles. Glitch breaks the image. V H S adds tape noise. R G B split separates colors. Scanlines resemble old screens. Pixelate turns letters into chunky pixels; mosaic forms blocks. Motion blur leaves a trail. Speed ramp changes timing. Parallax shifts layers at different depths. Spotlight directs attention. Sparkle lights up the headline. Smoke puff makes a hazy reveal. Fireworks close with a colorful burst. All thirty-three effects transform the words, not the character.
